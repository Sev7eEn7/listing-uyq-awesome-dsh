# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We aim to maintain a welcoming, professional, and technically focused community.

Examples of expected behavior:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus on technical merit.
- Welcome different experience levels.
- Provide actionable feedback.
- Respect project maintainers and contributors.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, spam, malicious submissions, and deliberate disruption.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
